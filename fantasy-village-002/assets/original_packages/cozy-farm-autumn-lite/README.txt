Cozy Farm Autumn Lite — autumn tiles for Cozy Farm (16 x 16, top-down)

69 PNG files (1x, 2x and 4x, nearest-neighbor, transparent background):
6 autumn grass tiles, the autumn path autotile, the maple, the autumn apple tree,
an autumn bush and a leaf pile. Made to match Cozy Farm Lite.

The full Cozy Farm Seasons adds autumn soil and animated water, the whole winter
set (snow, ice, snowy buildings, fences and props) and example maps:
https://aquumgifts.itch.io/cozy-farm-seasons

Made in code and text art with a fixed palette. No AI image generators were used.
